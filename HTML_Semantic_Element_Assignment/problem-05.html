Event Overview

The National Career and Education Fair 2026 is a large event designed for students, graduates, job seekers and professionals.

The fair brings together universities, colleges, companies, training institutes and career organizations under one platform.

Visitors can explore educational programs, career opportunities, internships, scholarships and placement opportunities.

The event will provide opportunities to interact directly with representatives from participating organizations.

Event Date: 15 October 2026

Event Time: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

Location: India Expo Centre, Greater Noida

Registration is open for students and job seekers.

Objectives of the Fair

The main objective of the fair is to connect students and job seekers with educational institutions and employers.

  • Provide career guidance to students.
  • Introduce visitors to different educational programs.
  • Provide information about internships.
  • Connect job seekers with employers.
  • Provide scholarship information.
  • Help students understand career opportunities.
  • Allow organizations to meet potential candidates.
  • Promote skill development and professional growth.

Preparation Guidelines

Visitors should prepare before attending the fair to make the most of the available opportunities.

  • Carry multiple copies of your updated resume.
  • Bring academic certificates when appropriate.
  • Prepare a short introduction about yourself.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Prepare questions about courses and jobs.
  • Dress appropriately for professional meetings.
  • Keep your identification documents available.
  • Arrive early to avoid long queues.
  • Keep a notebook for important information.

Preparation Tip

Prepare a simple introduction that explains your education, skills, interests and career goals.

Tip: Ask meaningful questions instead of only collecting brochures.

Attendee Journey

Follow these steps to make your visit organized and productive.

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive your registration confirmation.
  3. Arrive at the venue before your preferred sessions.
  4. Complete entry verification.
  5. Collect the event guide.

  8. Talk with recruiters and education representatives.

Career Fair Terminology

Career Fair
An event where employers and job seekers meet to discuss employment opportunities.
Internship
A temporary learning opportunity that provides practical workplace experience.
Placement
An opportunity where an organization recruits a candidate for employment.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students for education.
Apprenticeship
A structured training opportunity combining practical work and learning.

Important Visitor Information

Entry: Registered participants should carry their registration confirmation.

Identification: Visitors may be asked to show a valid identification document.

Timing: Participants should arrive at least thirty minutes before important sessions.

Dress Code: Professional or comfortable formal clothing is recommended for job seekers.

Documents: Students should carry resumes, academic details and relevant certificates.

Food: Food and refreshments may be available at designated areas.

Photography: Event photography may be conducted for promotional purposes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend the fair?

Students, graduates, job seekers, professionals and anyone interested in career or education opportunities can attend.

Is registration required?

Yes. Visitors are encouraged to complete registration before attending the event.

Should students carry resumes?

Yes. Students and job seekers should carry updated resumes when meeting employers.

Can students learn about scholarships?

Yes. Participating educational organizations will provide information about available scholarships.

Can I attend multiple sessions?

Yes. Participants can attend multiple sessions according to the event schedule and available seating.
