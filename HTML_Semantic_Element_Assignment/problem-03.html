Patient Instructions

Before Your Appointment

  • Carry a valid identification document.
  • Bring previous medical reports if available.
  • Carry your current prescription if applicable.
  • Arrive 15 minutes before your appointment.
  • Inform the clinic about any relevant allergies.

Appointment Process

  1. Select a department.

  5. Submit the appointment request.
  6. Wait for appointment confirmation.

Important Terms

Consultation
A professional discussion between a patient and healthcare provider about general health concerns.
Follow-up
A later appointment to review progress or continue care.
OPD
Outpatient Department where patients receive consultation without being admitted to the clinic.
Appointment
A scheduled time reserved for a patient consultation.
