Student Terms & Rules:

General Rules:

Students must carry their college ID card at all times.This helps maintain campus security.Students must maintain proper discipline and respectful behaviour.

Examination:

Students must follow all examination rules and instructionsMobile phones and other unauthorized electronic devices are not permitted during examinations.Violation may result in cancellation of the examination

Assignment Submission:

All assignments must be submitted before the given deadline.Late submissions may receive a penalty.

For example, assignments must be submitted by 25 August 2026.Students should follow the submission instructions provided by the teacher.

Discipline:

Students must maintain discipline inside classrooms and on campus.

Any form of harassment, violence, or damage to college property is strictly prohibited.Serious violations may lead to disciplinary action.

Library Rules:

Students must maintain silence in the library.

Books should be returned on or before the due date.
