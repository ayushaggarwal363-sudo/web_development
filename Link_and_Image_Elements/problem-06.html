TechFest 2026

Introduction

Welcome to TechFest 2026, the annual technology festival of our college. It is a platform where students can explore technology, innovation, programming and new ideas.
Students from different colleges can participate in exciting technical activities, competitions and workshops.

Highlights

TechFest 2026 includes coding competitions, robotics demonstrations, gaming events, technical quizzes and project exhibitions.

Date and Venue

Date: 15 September 2026
Time: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Venue: College Main Auditorium and Computer Labs

Speakers

The event will feature experienced technology professionals, software developers and entrepreneurs who will share their knowledge and career experiences with students. Guest speakers will also discuss Artificial Intelligence, Web Development, Cyber Security and future technologies.
