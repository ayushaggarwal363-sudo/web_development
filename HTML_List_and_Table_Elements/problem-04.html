Laptop Comparison Guide

Compare popular laptops based on their specifications, features, price and suitability to choose the right laptop for your needs.

Buying Factors

  • Processor
  • RAM
  • Storage
  • Display
  • Battery
  • Warranty

How to Choose Your Laptop

  1. Decide your main purpose for buying a laptop.
  2. Choose a suitable processor.
  3. Select the required RAM and storage.
  4. Check display quality and size.
  5. Compare battery capacity.
  6. Check the warranty and price.

Important Terms

RAM
Memory used by the laptop to run applications and tasks.
SSD
Fast storage used to store files and programs.
CPU
The processor that performs calculations and runs instructions.
GPU
A processor designed mainly for graphics and visual processing.
Refresh Rate
The number of times the display updates per second.
Battery Capacity
The amount of energy the laptop battery can store.
