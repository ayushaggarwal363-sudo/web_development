Annual College Fest 2026

Fest Introduction

Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day annual fest featuring competitions, events, prizes and activities for students.

Fest Information

The fest includes competitions, registration activities, awards and opportunities for students to participate and showcase their talents.

Registration Process

  1. Choose the event in which you want to participate.
  2. Fill in the registration form.
  3. Submit the required details.
  4. Pay the registration fee, if applicable.
  5. Receive the registration confirmation.

General Rules

  • Students must carry their college identity card.
  • Participants must report before the event starts.
  • Participants must follow the instructions of coordinators.
  • Any form of unfair practice is not allowed.
  • Decisions of the judges will be final.

Important Terms

Open Event
An event open for eligible students to participate.
Team Event
An event in which participants compete as a team.
Individual Event
An event in which a participant competes individually.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for a fest event.
Coordinator
The person responsible for managing an event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where finalists compete.
