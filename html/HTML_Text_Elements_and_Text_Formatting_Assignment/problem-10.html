GLA University Greater Noida:

GLA University Greater Noida is an industry-integrated campus located at Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida. The campus focuses on practical learning, industry exposure, innovation, and career development.

About University:

GLA University was established with a vision of providing quality education and developing skilled professionals. The Greater Noida campus focuses on industry-integrated education and practical learning.

Departments:

The Greater Noida campus offers programs in areas such as Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Electronics and Computer Engineering, Business Administration, and Computer Applications.
The academic environment combines faculty guidance with industry-oriented learning to help students develop technical and professional skills.

students can choose from programs including B.Tech CSE, B.Tech CSE with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, B.Tech Electronics and Computer Engineering, AI-enabled BBA, MBA, and MCA in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
